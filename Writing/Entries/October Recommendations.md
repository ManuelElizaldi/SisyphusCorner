October came and went like the falling leaves in the tree. It has been a constant in my 29 years of life that October feels like the beginning of a new beginning. 

The crisp air blowing around starts to smell different, it cools down the weather 

For some reason, like a super computer needing to be chilled down to work properly, I feel like I function better in this weather. This colder weather brings a sense of holiday with it. Houses start to dress up. You see monster decorations, which then become fall/Thanks giving decoration to end in a show of Christmas lights. 

My Birthday starts to loom closer and closer (Sagittarius gang!) and the weather starts to cool down. 

October also marks the start of a tradition I've been trying to keep alive since around 2007. Back then one of my greatest pleasures was to watch [The Angry Video Game Nerd](https://www.youtube.com/@JamesNintendoNerd). His main jazz was doing reviews of very bad old video games. But from time to time, he would entertain his audience with this reviews of cult classic horror films. 

He had an event where he did a movie review for each day of October (*need to check if my memory is right*), and he would recommend spooky films. This inspired the film marathon I do each year. 

I try to watch as many spooky films as I can, back when responsibilities were less and time more abundant I would watch 1 or more per day. Now, it becomaes harder but still I try to indulge in the spookyness. 

This month was a blessing in this regard, and since now I am sharing my experiences with the people of the interwebs, I wanted to share some of the best things I have enjoyed during this past month.

Spooky month is over, but that doesn't mean the spooky stops.

Starting off strong with Lee Cronin's The Mummy. I thought this movie was going to be a cheap refried version of the action packed classics from the 2000s - The Mummy Returns & The Scorpion King. I watched this movie in the worst possible place one could watch a scary movie: A plane. What can I say? I like to keep myself on my toes. 

This movie mixes the anguish a family goes through from having their daughter kidnapped and the helplessness dread from having her consumed by an ancient Egyptian curse. With a lovely sprinkle of body horror. 

This is what The Exorcists would've been like if the possessing demon was born in Egypt. It manages suspense really well and there are some scenes in there that made my skin tingle with anxiety. I have to give props to the SFX team on this one as well.

Doing a bit of research for this article I found out that there's also a couple of 1940s 'The Mummy' movies I plan to watch some other time!

I remember watching this movie when it was first released, and I was scared but not as these so-called scientists claimed. Also, what type of scientist do you have to be in order to test the scariness level of a movie? Anyway, the movie follows Ellison, a true-crime writer plagued with writer's block.
