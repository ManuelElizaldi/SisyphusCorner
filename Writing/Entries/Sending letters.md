The other day while browsing YouTube I found a curious video. It detailed one of the most sophisticated systems humans have ever built. Regardless of its complicated logistics, it has proved to be so resilient, that it has run for more than 250 years.

For only 82 cents it has connected people across an entire country. I have used this system to share my love in text to my girlfriend, my parents and friends. It has also delivered bad news, glorious news and everybody, once in a while received unwanted junk. 

This system sees no color, no race, no ethnicity. It values someone living in the tip of Alaska or the bottom of a canyon the same as someone wearing spurs, riding a horse and wearing cowboy hat in west Texas.

If there has even been a manifestation of democracy, this is it.
