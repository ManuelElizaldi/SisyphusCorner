I woke up early, chugged the water I had left in my bed side table and marched onward to start my Saturday. I turned on ol'Bertha, my espresso machine and gave her a couple of seconds to heat up. Every time I start her up you can hear something going on inside of it rattling and making all sorts of noise. 

Right now I am using a blend of coffee I bought in a recent trip I made to Mexico City. The beans were roasted by Kiyo in Roma Norte. This blend comes from Oaxaca. Betraying my well honed habit of drinking medium roasts, lately I have been gravitating towards lighter roasts. Some so-and-so coffee expert once told me that lighter roasts allow the taste of the beans to show up more in the cup. I don't think that is why I bought this light roast, I just wanted to switch things up. 

Continuing with the ritual, I ground up the coffee a bit coarser than usual. I have been getting better extractions this way. Then I prepared my puck and again, I was blessed by the coffee gods because I got some caramel crema floating on top of my caffeine.
