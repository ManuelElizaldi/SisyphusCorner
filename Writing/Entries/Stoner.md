My objective with this piece is to write a bit about Stoner, some initial thoughts and attempt at answering the question "What did this book do to me?"

I have to begin at the prose of this book. I was mesmerized by how simple but elegant John William's writing is. I fear that if I ever tried to replicate his style I would probably just faint in revulsion at the atrocious words I would write. His style is elegant without being pretentious, I can clearly notice the years of experience in how sew his ideas into words. 

One example of this prose is when he describes what love is for our character Stoner. We follow Stoner throughout his attempt at love, like icraus flying too close to the sun / but never quite reaching it in his own household  he discovered love and then experiences the disappointment of not having his love is not mutual. Then Williams lays down one of the best descriptions of what is love. 

Love for knowledge - I, like Stoner, truly enjoy the pleasure of reading, learning new things.
